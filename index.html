A web designer who became an AI engineer the long way around.

I fell into web design in college, survived a multi-year tour rebuilding a Shopify storefront, and now spend my days wiring AI agents into real workflows at Gabb Wireless.

My actual specialty is closing the gap between "wouldn't it be cool if AI could…" and a thing that runs in production. I've built Claude-powered agents across multiple departments, shipped an FTC-compliance tool that reads marketing copy and flags legal risk before the lawyers have to, and explained prompt engineering to people who'd just watched a documentary about Skynet. I write TypeScript and React on the front, Node and Python on the back, and I'm fluent in the one language every AI tool actually runs on: clear instructions.

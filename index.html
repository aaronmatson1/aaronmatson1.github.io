Gatekeeper and the Kidernet

Home network filtering

Running at home

Internet for kids that starts closed and opens on purpose. Allowlist-only DNS filtering with Pi-hole in a Proxmox LXC container. Khan Academy, Scratch, and Chrome Music Lab are in. Everything else is out until it's approved.

The concept adds a tiered "Digital Learner's Permit" for earning access, and the Kidernet, a curated offline content layer. Started as a hackathon pitch at Gabb.
